The United Kingdom (UK) is a country made up of four nations: England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. It sits off the north-western coast of mainland Europe and has a long history that has shaped global politics, culture, and economics. The UK is a constitutional monarchy with a parliamentary system, combining a ceremonial monarch with an elected government. Its capital, London, is a major world city known for finance, culture, and innovation. Overall, the UK plays a significant role internationally while maintaining a diverse cultural identity across its four parts.
