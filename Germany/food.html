Food & Cuisine

Hearty Dishes, Bread Culture, and Brewing Mastery

Typical Dishes & Specialties

Schnitzel and Bread

A Schnitzel is a thin slice of meat fried in fat, often breaded (Wiener Schnitzel). German bread culture is massive, boasting over 300 types of bread. The **Pretzel (Brezel)**, a baked pastry shaped into a knot, is a Bavarian staple often served with Obatzda cheese spread.

Regional Favorites

Regional foods are diverse. **Spätzle** (a type of soft egg pasta) is typical of the Swabian region. **Currywurst** (sausage with curry ketchup) is a fast-food favorite originating in Berlin. And, of course, the ubiquitous **Bratwurst** is found everywhere, differing slightly by region.

German Beer Culture

Beer is a major part of German culture. German beer is brewed according to the **Reinheitsgebot** (Purity Order) of 1516, which permits only water, hops, and malt as ingredients (yeast was added later). This commitment to quality makes German beer world-renowned.

  • Pilsner: Pale lager with a bitter taste. Most common across Germany.
  • Weizenbier: Refreshing wheat beer, popular in the South.
  • Kölsch: A specialty beer brewed only in Cologne.
