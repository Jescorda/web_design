Tourist Destinations

Fairytales, Nature, and Romantic Roads

Neuschwanstein Castle

A 19th-century historicist palace in the Bavarian Alps. It was commissioned by King Ludwig II of Bavaria as a retreat and in homage to Richard Wagner. It is one of the most famous castles in the world and was the inspiration for Disney's Sleeping Beauty castle.

The Black Forest (Schwarzwald)

A large forested mountain range in southwest Germany. Known for its dense, evergreen forests and picturesque villages, it is often associated with the Brothers Grimm fairy tales. It is also the home of the cuckoo clock and the famous Black Forest cake.

The Rhine Valley

The Middle Rhine is a UNESCO World Heritage site, famous for its wines, vineyards, and over 40 medieval castles perched on hilltops overlooking the river. Taking a boat cruise down the Rhine is a quintessential German experience.

Rothenburg ob der Tauber

Located on the "Romantic Road," Rothenburg is Germany's best-preserved medieval walled town. Walking through its cobblestone lanes feels like stepping back into the Middle Ages. The town is famous for its Christmas shop (Käthe Wohlfahrt).
