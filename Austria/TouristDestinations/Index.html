About Tourist Destinations

Austria's attractions include royal history, alpine scenery, and charming small towns. Visitors can enjoy both cultural landmarks and breathtaking natural landscapes.

Schönbrunn Palace

A grand imperial residence known for its lavish rooms and vast gardens. It offers a glimpse into Austria's royal history and baroque elegance.

Grossglockner High Alpine Road

A scenic mountain road with dramatic views of Austria's highest peak.

Krimml Waterfalls

Austria's tallest waterfalls, surrounded by forest and mountain paths. It's a stunning natural highlight.

Melk Abbey

A magnificent baroque monastery overlooking the river Danube. It's famed for its golden library, grand halls, and peaceful setting.

Lake Neusiedl

A shallow lake known for sailing, cycling, and wildlife. Its sunny climate and relaxed atmosphere make it a popular getaway.
