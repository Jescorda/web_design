About food

In Austria, you can find many delicious meals, such as Wiener Schnitzel, Tafelspitz, Sachertorte, Kaiserschmarrn, and Apfelstrudel.

Wiener Schnitzel

A thin, crispy breaded veal cutlet that's one of Austria's most iconic dishes. It's simple, golden, and traditionally served with lemon and potato salad.

Tafelspitz

Tender boiled beef served in broth with classic sides like horseradish and apple sauce. It's a comforting, elegant selection within the Austrian cuisine.

Sachertorte

A rich chocolate cake with a thin layer of apricot jam and a glossy chocolate glaze. It's famous worldwide for its dense, refined flavor.

Kaiserschmarrn

A fluffy shredded pancake caramelised with sugar and served with fruit compote. Light, sweet, and perfect as a dessert or meal.

Apfelstrudel

A delicate pastry filled with spiced apples and raisins, wrapped in thin, flaky dough. It's one of Austria's most beloved traditional desserts.
