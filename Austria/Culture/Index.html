Introducing Austria's Culture

Discover traditions, festivals, and more

About culture

Austrian culture values tradition, craftsmanship, and a relaxed lifestyle. Regional customs and hospitality are important.

Music

Austria has a strong classical music tradition shaped by Mozart and Strauss. Concerts, festivals, and folk music remain central to cultural life.

Literature

Austrian literature mixes deep reflection with sharp social insight. Themes often focus on identity and history.

Architecture

Austria blends imperial palaces and Baroque churches with clean, modern design. Historic and contemporary styles coexist everywhere.

Art

Austrian art is bold and expressive, influenced by Klimt and Schiele. Museums showcase both classical and modern works.

Explore more...

Austria offers mountains, historic towns, and lively festivals. There's always something new to discover.
