About Austria

Austria is a landlocked country in Central Europe, lying in the Eastern Alps with Vienna being the capital city. Austria is bordered by Germany to the northwest, the Czech Republic to the north, Slovakia to the northeast, Hungary to the east, Slovenia and Italy to the south, and Switzerland and Liechtenstein to the west. The country occupies an area of 83,879 km squared and has a population of around 9 million. German is the official language spoken by natives.

Vienna

Vienna, the capital of Austria, is known as the "City of Music" for being the home of classical composers like Mozart and Beethoven, as well as its imperial architecture.

Graz

Graz is Austria's second-largest city, known for its old towns and modern arts.

Salzburg

Salzburg is best known as the birthplace of Mozart. It is also the setting for the famous film 'The Sound of Music'.

Linz

Linz is a city along the river Danube, known for art, technology, and cultural festivals.

Innsbruck

Innsbruck is a city in the Alps, famous for skiing, mountain scenery, and historical buildings.
