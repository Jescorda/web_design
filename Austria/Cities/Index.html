About Austria

Austria is a beautiful country in Central Europe, known for music, art, and mountains.

Vienna

Vienna, the capital of Austria, is famous for its imperial palaces, classical music, and historic architecture.

Graz

Graz is Austria's second-largest city, known for its Renaissance old town and modern art scene.

Salzburg

Salzburg is best known as the birthplace of Mozart and the setting for the Sound of Music.

Linz

Linz is a city along the Danube, known for art, technology, and cultural festivals.

Innsbruck

Innsbruck is a city in the Alps, famous for skiing, mountain scenery, and historical buildings.
