Eurovison: Made in Switzerland

Switzerland, a landlocked country at the crossroads of Central, Western, and Southern Europe, bordered by Germany, France, Italy, Austria, and Liechtenstein. A federal republic of 26 cantons founded August 1 1291 with an estimated population of 9million. Switzerland is known for its political stability varied landscapes, cultural richness and a mix of traditonal and modern lifestyles. It has four national languages —German, French, Italian, and Romanish— its deep tradition of multilingualism and diverse regional identities
